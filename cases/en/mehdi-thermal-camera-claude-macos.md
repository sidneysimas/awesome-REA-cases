# Thermal camera: Mehdi's Claude-assisted Mac app and a proposed Seek XR extension

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 05:52 Beijing time on October 19, @MehdiHacks says he only prompted Claude, had no SDK, used forum reversing information about other models and had Claude enumerate the device live. The 05:30 parent concerns a lab thermal camera supported only on Windows and Android, for which he made a native macOS app with Claude. An intervening question asked whether he reversed USB or wrapped an SDK; the reply explicitly rules out an SDK.

Forum work must not be recast as Claude independently discovering a protocol, and live enumeration does not establish complete protocol compatibility. The original device's brand and model, source, USB captures and temperature-accuracy comparisons remain unavailable. There was no independent device test and REA use is unconfirmed.

At 21:26 on October 19, @W00fer proposes using Claude to decompile the Android APK and investigate whether Mehdi's app could support their device. Earlier replies in the retrieved conversation identify it as a Seek Thermal XR: W00fer requested support at 07:22, and Mehdi said at 17:22 that he does not own one. The reader cannot sacrifice the device for debugging, making APK research a proposal to reduce physical trial-and-error, not completed analysis. Seek XR is not a confirmed identification of Mehdi's original camera, and no compatible implementation or successful flashing is reported. Earlier replies supplement context rather than becoming new candidates.
