# Smart toilet from the desktop

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-12

thinkszyg wanted to control their own smart toilet from a computer. The author used REA and Codex to analyze a WeChat mini-program's Bluetooth communication and says a computer status-bar control for opening and closing the lid was built in about half an hour. In this review window, the author adds that control has expanded to other home devices, managed together in the Mac status bar, without listing every device model or protocol. Both are firsthand usage reports, not independently retested.
