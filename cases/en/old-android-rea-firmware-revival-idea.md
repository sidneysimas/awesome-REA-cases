# Old Android phones: an REA custom-firmware revival question

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-18
Stage: Needs verification

At 17:58 Beijing time on October 18, a reader asks whether anyone has used REA to reverse-engineer Android phone firmware and make custom firmware to give older phones a second life, mentioning morluto. Unlike the old-iPhone iOS idea, the target is Android device longevity.

No model, SoC, firmware version, broken feature, boot chain, model choice or implementation is identified. Another candidate reply says source is already available without specifying a component, which cannot establish that drivers or complete device firmware are open source. This is only a question and idea, not proof REA can bypass a locked boot chain or put new systems on every old phone.
