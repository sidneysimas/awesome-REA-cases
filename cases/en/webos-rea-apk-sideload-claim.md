Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA

Stage: Needs verification

At 11:50 Beijing time on October 20, @Devcop101 says they used REA to sideload APKs onto a webOS TV and remove all factory bloatware, making the TV fast. The concrete goal is reducing preinstalled software and adding apps, but the source mixes Android's APK format with webOS. This technical wording remains unresolved and is not presented as a general working procedure or grounds to infer the TV's actual OS.

No model, OS version, package, commands, execution log, before/after measurement or inspectable code is provided. 'All' software and improved speed are author claims, not established safe removal or performance gains. Firmware reversing, rooting and an Android compatibility layer are not established either. This collection did not sideload, remove TV software or test devices. The account is retained as an unverified claim with a concrete goal and an explicit platform inconsistency.
