# LG air conditioner: bot-assisted research toward fully local control without initial app login

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-20
Stage: Needs verification

At 01:59 Beijing time on October 20, a respondent recommends Home Assistant as the home-control backbone and bots for devices without integrations. They say their bot reverse-engineered LG air conditioners that week. Their existing integration already worked; the new aim is 100% local operation without the manufacturer's app even for initial login, not repairing an entirely nonfunctional integration.

No model, firmware, protocol, bot/model identity, code, initial-pairing or offline tests are supplied. The post states a research activity and goal, not proof that full cloud independence was achieved. REA is unconfirmed. This is separate from TCL, Samsung and Tuya air-conditioner records; no device was connected.
