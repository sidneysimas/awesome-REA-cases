# iriver E30: personalizing an unsupported player's main screen

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-15
Stage: In progress

After iriver stopped supporting a personally owned E30 player, @MikeS190477 began reverse-engineering the device to modify its main screen. The original explicitly says work is ongoing and presents AI as a possible future route to device personalization. Rereading the original confirmed the author and wording, without protocols, firmware version or flashing-test records. Expectations about AI are not recast as firmware reconstruction already completed by a model. This is a concrete old-device maintenance project; REA and the actual AI toolchain remain unknown.

REA use unconfirmed; main-screen modification is not complete firmware recovery.
