# UniXNU's Sony BRAVIA tablet port: MrWhite relays mostly unattended AI-written UEFI adaptation

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-25
Stage: In progress

At 18:07:12 Beijing time on October 25, 2026, MrWhite (@neuronxwhite) reports in a UniXNU device-port roundup that a Sony BRAVIA tablet port is in progress, with its author having AI write most of the UEFI adaptation unattended. The context is native iPadOS/macOS boot on non-Apple ARM64 hardware through UEFI, DarwinBoot and a modified XNU, rather than virtualization. AI participation is explicitly attributed only to the Sony tablet's UEFI adaptation, not every device, kernel or GPU driver.

The same post lists the Xiaomi Pad 6S Pro, Adreno 740, other phones and tablets, and records marking 13 subsystems implemented. These are secondhand project-context claims, not acceptance of the Sony tablet's complete functions, hardware boot or GPU acceleration. The post also warns that camera and Bluetooth are not working and the work remains in progress. The exact Sony model, original developer, AI model, reversing material, code and unattended sessions were not obtained. A device category and status table do not establish everyday usability, compatibility or safe flashing.

Retained as an in-progress secondary report of a named porting direction. The claims of 921 stars, more than 40 device repositories, a 208 MiB payload and licensing were not verified.
