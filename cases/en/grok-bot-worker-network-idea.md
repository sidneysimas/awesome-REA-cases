# Grok Bot: a worker-network reverse-engineering idea not pursued

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: Needs verification

The author reports Grok Bot could no longer connect to a local Mac after an automatic update. After troubleshooting with several AI tools, the author concluded the connection worker did not follow network-egress rules. Gemini proposed decompiling the app and modifying that process's network behavior, but the author considered it unnecessary and instead used Clash TUN mode, reporting restored connectivity. This records an unimplemented AI reverse-engineering suggestion and troubleshooting context, not a located or fixed binary defect. The network explanation is also the author's judgment without packet captures or code locations.
