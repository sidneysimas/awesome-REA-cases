# Raw NMR/LC-MS files: an unverified report of Claude format parsing and purity estimation

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-21
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:00 Beijing time on October 21, 2026, lifcc (mylifcc) recounts an instrument-data investigation within a post about Anthropic scientific work. Claude Opus 5 reportedly received outsourced-laboratory raw NMR and LC-MS files without format identification and only two sentences of instruction, then wrote code to parse the instrument formats. The goal is recovering spectral processing and compound purity from raw data, not the protein-design work described elsewhere in the post.

The account claims Fourier transformation, phase and baseline correction and integration, with 96.4% purity in 19 minutes versus the professional laboratory's 96.33%, plus a suggestion for a D2O exchange experiment to confirm labile hydrogens. All figures and operations remain secondary claims. Original-post retrieval confirms the author and text, but the claimed primary Anthropic report, instrument vendor, formats, compound, raw files and scripts were not obtained. Blind testing, significance of the difference, general parsing capability and timing are not established. Protein wet-lab results do not validate this format investigation; REA is not stated.
