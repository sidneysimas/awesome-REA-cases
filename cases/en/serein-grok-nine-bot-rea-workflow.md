# Serein's nine-bot workflow: pass REA behavior specifications to an isolated builder

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-17
Stage: Needs verification

At 15:54 Beijing time on October 17, 2026, a Chinese post recounts a weekly product-development workflow with nine specialist Grok bots, saying the original author's Mac browser Serein was built with it. The reverse-engineering stage connects REA as a custom MCP in Grok's cloud computer. A Behavior Reverser extracts only user-action → internal-steps → result chains; a Spec Writer turns these into SPEC.md; a fully isolated Builder is then meant to write code and automated tests, with private source-code export prohibited. The goal is to turn competitor-feature research into input for an independent implementation.

The account also describes file/HANDOFF.md exchanges, downstream work starting only after an upstream DONE marker, and customer complaints, strategy and distribution metrics deciding whether to continue. These are the source's descriptions of templates and author practice, not this collection deploying nine bots or verifying isolation. The original long-form article, Serein repository, concrete competitor targets, test outcomes and execution logs were not checked. A clean-room label does not prove isolation or legal compliance, and scheduling templates are not evidence of continuous operation.
