# Urban Terror in the browser

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-12

Urban Terror is a multiplayer shooter based on Quake III technology. levelsio had tried bringing it to the browser with ioquake3 and WebAssembly, but could not get it running. In a new attempt, the model located data-layout differences between engine structures and game bytecode. After correcting them, the author reports that the game ran in the browser and connected to multiplayer servers, with a gameplay video. Reverse engineering investigated low-level compatibility blocking the port rather than recovering the whole game from scratch.
