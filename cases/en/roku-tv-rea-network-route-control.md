# Roku OS TV: REA-assisted network-route discovery and control

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-20
Stage: Needs verification

At 11:01 Beijing time on October 20, @greco_dev says Roku OS TV control is also finished and '100% functional'. The author immediately clarifies that, for technical readers, this is not really reverse engineering: they find the TV's local-network IP, send requests to its routes, and use the REA repository to help map those routes. The concrete use is device-interface discovery leading to control, not source recovery from firmware.

The post supplies no TV model, Roku version, endpoint list, inventory of working controls or inspectable test output. The 100% statement describes the author's setup, not compatibility across models. Linked material is not used to infer ad removal, firmware flashing or a standalone client. This collection did not connect to a TV or send requests; this remains an author-reported interoperability account.
