# eFootball: AI reports of accuracy/speed thresholds and linear Physical Contact mechanics

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-23
Stage: Needs verification

At 01:05 Beijing time on October 20, S0LOMUN claims AI reverse-engineered eFootball's Accuracy Coefficient for passing and shooting to guide training. They report large per-point gains from 60 to 90, approximately 40% lower returns above 90, and applicability to Passing, Finishing and Heading, interpreting 90 as the developers' intended threshold.

The same day's 03:34 reply and its conversation confirm eFootball and researcher attribution: S0LOMUN credits Amadeusz and Wyzzz for skill/stat research and says they receive updates. A 06:38 post says wyzzzzzzz's AI reversing also finds a Speed threshold of 90 and roughly 40% lower returns above it, but the diminishing returns Amadeusz measured between 96 and 99 in August do not appear in the AI decompilation results. The methods are not thereby shown to agree, nor does this mean there is no improvement above 90.

A new October 23 post at 05:39:35 attributes AI research of Physical Contact to wyzzzzzzz, claiming no thresholds at 80, 85, 90 or 95 and completely linear scaling. Turning while shielding allegedly uses one's own stat; close back-to-opponent shielding compares both players' Physical Contact; side-by-side dribbling compares Physical Contact plus Balance. Under-pressure passing/shooting reduces ball-speed and accuracy penalties; aerial duels compare Physical Contact, Heading and Jumping, with height and Aerial Superiority bonuses. This adds a report about another attribute and scenarios, neither overturning earlier Speed/Accuracy claims nor proving one formula covers every situation.

No formula, game version, binary records, sample count, raw accuracy/speed/contact data or controlled experiment is supplied. Developer intent lacks first-party evidence. Neither model nor REA is identified. This remains a mechanism-research retelling, not an official training rule or independent verification. The original 01:05 candidate has 64 likes, 2,779 views and 1 repost; the 06:38 candidate has 14 likes, 803 views and 0 reposts. These remain October 20 circulation snapshots, not refreshed to October 23.
