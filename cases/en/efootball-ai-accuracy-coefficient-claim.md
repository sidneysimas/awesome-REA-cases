# eFootball: AI-inferred accuracy and speed thresholds of 90, with a manual-measurement disagreement

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-20
Stage: Needs verification

At 01:05 Beijing time on October 20, S0LOMUN claims AI reverse-engineered eFootball's Accuracy Coefficient for passing and shooting to guide training. They report large per-point gains from 60 to 90, approximately 40% lower returns above 90, and applicability to Passing, Finishing and Heading, interpreting 90 as the developers' intended threshold.

Separate review of the 03:34 reply and its conversation confirms eFootball as the target, resolving the earlier ambiguity caused by names alone. S0LOMUN attributes skill/stat research to Amadeusz and Wyzzz and says they receive updates. This is attribution context from the older window. A new 06:38 post says wyzzzzzzz's AI reversing also finds a Speed threshold of 90 and roughly 40% lower returns above it, but the diminishing returns Amadeusz measured between 96 and 99 in August do not appear in the AI decompilation results. The methods are not thereby shown to agree, nor does this mean there is no improvement above 90.

No formula, game version, binary records, sample count, raw accuracy/speed data or controlled experiment is supplied. Developer intent lacks first-party evidence. Neither model nor REA is identified. This remains a mechanism-research retelling, not an official training rule or independent verification. The original 01:05 candidate has 64 likes, 2,779 views and 1 repost; the 06:38 candidate has 14 likes, 803 views and 0 reposts, all October 20 circulation snapshots.
