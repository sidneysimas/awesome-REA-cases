# A colony-simulation engine: a decompilation research plan

Category: Games and preservation · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-15
Stage: In progress

@Promptmethus says they are building a colony simulation with AI-generated inhabitants and worlds, hoping players can enter and survive in a fully 3D world with destructible terrain. They say decompilation and reverse-engineering tools have been downloaded and a local GPU is available; next they plan to open existing game engines and inspect exact code to understand implementation. One target is written as 'room world' without a formal project name, version or binary; that spelling is not silently corrected into a confirmed target. This records a research plan with a specific purpose, not completed decompilation. It does not explain which analysis step AI has actually performed, and REA use is unconfirmed.

Tool preparation and development vision, not completed engine reconstruction.
