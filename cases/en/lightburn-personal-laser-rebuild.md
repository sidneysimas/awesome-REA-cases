# LightBurn: a reported personal laser-control rebuild

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-13
Stage: Needs verification

Homard Simpson (LIQUID8RPF) reports using Reverse Engineer Anything to make LightBurn-like software for a personal laser machine in about 16 minutes, emphasizing only the needed features. xreach confirmed the account and two attached images exist, but the images were not viewed and no laser device was controlled. No laser model, studied part of the original program, command protocol, source, machining result or safety limitations are disclosed. Generating a software interface is not evidence of real hardware compatibility, much less a complete LightBurn replica.
