# CS2 × Minecraft: a promotional REA account of Dust2, bows and heart-based health

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:58 Beijing time on October 19, a promotion claims REA and Opus 5.5 read Counter-Strike 2 and Minecraft together, turning Dust2 into blocks, health into hearts and weapons into bows, linking movement to Minecraft's player tick. It claims 3.92 million bytes scanned, 18,204 named functions, 49 builds, all 16 rules passing, 214 files compiled, 60 fps and 12 ms latency. The intended use is combining two games' mechanics into a new scene.

No sample versions, code, SPEC, rule list, input comparison or performance sampling is supplied. A player_move/EntityPlayer.tick screen annotation does not establish connected client implementations. All numbers remain promotional self-reporting, not real benchmarks or a verified delivered playable game. Minecraft sales and Dust2's age are not project engagement. No independent execution was performed; this differs from the existing Minecraft/PvZ target.
