# CapCut subtitles: a template-driven renderer idea

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-13
Stage: Needs verification

Replying to 宝玉's analysis of CapCut's animated subtitles, byeval says they manually studied the rendering engine before the AI era: it uses shaders and SDF text, with styling and animation systems. The author proposes having AI now reconstruct a renderer from the template DSL, using templates as a test set for image diffs. The historical manual research is not an AI result, and the current AI approach remains an idea. It is separate from others' CapCut editor or transition projects.

Stage: concrete idea; historical reverse engineering was manual
