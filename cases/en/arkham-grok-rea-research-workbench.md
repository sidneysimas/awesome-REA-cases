# Arkham: a Grok Bot/REA research-workbench rebuild

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: In progress

Arkham analyzes on-chain fund flows and wallet activity. The poster describes using Grok Bot and REA to investigate its interface behavior, then customize a workbench around personal wallet watchlists, transfer filters, alerts and research views. The post says the footage shows an agent researching and drafting the reconstruction and explicitly places real-data interaction testing as the next step. This is ongoing interface-behavior research, not acquired proprietary Arkham data, all alerts implemented or a trading-ready system. No public finished product, data-connection acceptance or independent execution results are supplied.
