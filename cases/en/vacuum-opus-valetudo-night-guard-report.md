At 20:00:03 Beijing time on October 28, 2026, Martin (@0x_meden) says Opus 5.5 helped turn a rooted vacuum with a camera, lidar and wheels into a night-patrol device. Claude Code first checks whether the exact model supports Valetudo and local camera access, stopping if not.
