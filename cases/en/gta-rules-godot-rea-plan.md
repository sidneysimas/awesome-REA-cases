# GTA rules into Godot: an REA plan with unclear target version

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: In progress

The author says they are studying GTA with REA and Opus 5.5, planning to locate speed, driving and wanted-level functions, use runtime observation to distinguish OBSERVED, INFERRED and UNKNOWN, then rebuild with Godot and the author's own assets and write rule tests. The post opens with GTA6 investment and says progress will be shared until its release, but never identifies which GTA title or version is actually available for study. The unspecified target is not upgraded to possession and decompilation of GTA6, nor is one minute of observation proof of complete rules. This is an open-world research plan with concrete gameplay categories but unclear version and results, without code, locations or test records.
