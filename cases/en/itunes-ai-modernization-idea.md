# iTunes: an AI-modernization idea for old-iPod music workflows

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: Needs verification

After seeing AI software-reconstruction stories, the poster asks whether the approach could be applied to iTunes to update and optimize it for modern computing. Their motivation is a view that Apple abandoned a still-useful music player while interest in old iPods is returning. The concrete idea is preserving an older music-player and device workflow while improving the modern-system experience. No reverse engineering, implementation, protocol compatibility or device testing is reported. Claims about abandonment and renewed popularity remain the author's motivation, not independent market findings.
