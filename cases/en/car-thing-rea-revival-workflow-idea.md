# Spotify Car Thing: a hypothetical REA backup, firmware-mapping and community-revival workflow

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-21
Stage: Needs verification

At 00:57 Beijing time on October 21, 2026, an author uses Spotify Car Thing's shutdown to describe how they would have investigated their own device if Opus 5.5 and REA had been available then. The source gives December 9, 2024 as shutdown day and $90 as price; these are attributed historical details, not verified in this review.

The proposal reads public chip, USB boot-mode and superbird-tool documentation, backs up partitions and compares two copies by SHA256, then asks REA to map startup, phone communication and shutdown logic. It would export owner settings, compare Nocturne, DeskThing and GlanceThing licenses, maintenance and rollback, and prepare flashing/recovery checklists. The author requests linked evidence, file checks and explicit guesses.

This is a device-specific hypothetical learning workflow, not an executed backup, firmware crack or successful flash. The author explicitly credits earlier manual community work and says today's owner can simply install an existing project. There are no new firmware-analysis artifacts, device logs or independent tests. Rollback advice is not a guarantee against bricking, and unverified REA star-count marketing is not adopted.
