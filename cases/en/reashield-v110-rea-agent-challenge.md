# ReaShield v1.1.0: a five-level source-recovery challenge for rea-agents@6.7.0

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-25
Stage: Related project

At 09:26 Beijing time on October 21, 2026, Jacques Gariepy invited rea-agents@6.7.0 users to direct their agent to ReaShield v1.1.0's AGENTS.md and see whether it passes level one. The post advertises five levels and $100 for complete source recovery, naming Claude Code, Cursor and Codex.

At 18:47:48 on October 25, the author added a reply to the REA v6.8 announcement: targets include a JavaScript package and a Windows binary, with agents scored on recovered content up to 1,000 points and a $100 full-source recovery bounty. He says he attempted to reverse the challenge, but gives no score, model, recovered fragments or successful result. This item was reviewed from an earlier window; supplementary thread lookup confirmed the same author. The REA release itself is not treated as a new case.

The concrete purpose is adversarial testing of AI reversing, not proof of effective protection or impossibility of reversing. Available material does not provide an expanded challenge-code identity, full rules, submitted scores or independent tests. Levels, points and reward are publisher claims. An older 697/696-function promotional story in the supplementary thread is not a current-window result or a score on this challenge. This collection did not execute it.
