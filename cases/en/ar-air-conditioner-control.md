# Air-conditioner protocol for AR gesture control

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-12
Stage: Needs verification

HalfBit recounts an icanberk demonstration: run the air conditioner's official app in an Android emulator and observe requests, use REA to analyze the on/off and temperature-control protocol, then have Codex build an interface and gesture interactions in Lens Studio, an AR-glasses development platform. The aim is to control the device directly through glasses rather than rebuild the air-conditioner app. The circulating post claims about 15 minutes, without a device model, protocol specification or code. The demonstration does not establish compatibility with arbitrary home appliances.
