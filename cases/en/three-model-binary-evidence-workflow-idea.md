# Three-model binary research: function maps, competing hypotheses and an original-binary oracle

In the proposed division, Sonnet 5.5 maps function addresses, roles, evidence and confidence; Opus 5.5 gives difficult functions two competing interpretations and minimal distinguishing tests; Fable 5.1 is called when needed to find three high-impact failure modes. Every claim should be marked OBSERVED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN and backed by an address, cross-reference or test log. Reference behavior comes from the original binary rather than a model grading its own reconstruction.

The third model is reserved for surviving alternative interpretations, long dependency chains or a high-stakes pre-build review, aiming to avoid expensive-model work on tasks such as listing strings.
