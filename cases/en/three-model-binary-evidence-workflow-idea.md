# Three-model binary research: function maps, competing hypotheses and an original-binary oracle

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-18
Stage: Needs verification

At 17:30 Beijing time on October 17, 2026, a poster proposes a concrete binary-investigation workflow starting with a small, self-compiled samples/record.c lab fixture, not a claimed commercial-software rebuild. In the proposed division, Sonnet 5.5 maps function addresses, roles, evidence and confidence; Opus 5.5 gives difficult functions two competing interpretations and minimal distinguishing tests; Fable 5.1 is called when needed to find three high-impact failure modes. Every claim should be marked OBSERVED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN and backed by an address, cross-reference or test log. Reference behavior comes from the original binary rather than a model grading its own reconstruction.

The third model is reserved for surviving alternative interpretations, long dependency chains or a high-stakes pre-build review, aiming to avoid expensive-model work on tasks such as listing strings. The post quotes USD 2/10 per million input/output tokens for Sonnet, 4/20 for Opus and 10/50 for Fable, with a budget example of about USD 0.30/0.60/1.50 for 100K input plus 10K output tokens. These are the source's model names and pricing assumptions, not confirmed provider offerings, prices or actual bills.
