# Klipsch Lumina speakers: AI-assisted BLE research with webcam and microphone feedback

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-28
Stage: In progress

At 23:32:22 Beijing time on October 28, 2026, Pawan Deshpande (@idlivada) reports that Klipsch ProMedia Lumina 2.1 speakers have LED backlights but no Mac app and no published BLE protocol. AI studied sniffed traffic and mobile-app binaries to build a menu-bar controller. Initially he reported whether lights changed and EQ sounded right; he then supplied microphone and webcam input so the AI could check audio and LEDs, shortening the feedback loop.

He says the app shipped with an ambient mode matching the dominant screen color. The original has a video and a reply offers a project link.
