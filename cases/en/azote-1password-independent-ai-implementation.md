# An independent AI 1Password implementation: Azote's incomplete alternative after data loss

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-29
Stage: Needs verification

At 04:50:21 Beijing time on October 29, 2026, Azote (@AzoteFurry) reports losing a year of 1Password data during migration, suspecting the official migration webpage but acknowledging uncertainty. Local caches could, in the author's estimate, reduce the loss to the newest five days. Support reportedly could not restore data after departure from a family group. The post mentions prior 1Password 7 work and an independent AI reversing route; this entry concerns only the latter.

At 04:51:12, a same-author reply calls one project an independent implementation with incomplete functionality. The main post says both projects expose source but are not open source, and suggests possible use for clean-room development. These are author descriptions. Repository, license, cache parsing, encryption compatibility, recovery results and AI model were not verified. This does not establish successful recovery with only five days lost or safe password management.

At 06:41:48 in this window, ByteLee (@bytelee_trade) questions whether implementation by an independent AI is clean-room work if reversed source already entered model context, suggesting records of who saw what. This is a reader's methodological concern, not proof of contamination or a legal finding. No vault, account or implementation was accessed or executed here; REA is unspecified.
