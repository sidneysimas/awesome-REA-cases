# A wireless CarPlay adapter: a reported REA/Grok firmware change

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: Needs verification

A Turkish-language circulating post says Arif used REA to study an in-car wireless CarPlay adapter and modify firmware to fix automatic connection problems. After Claude said it could not complete the task, Grok reportedly produced a new binary in half an hour. The target is accessory connection behavior, not vehicle-control systems. The candidate supplies no original author research thread, device model, firmware diff, flashing record or reconnection tests. This is an unverified report with a clear device category and fault, not confirmation that the binary was safely flashed or that every scenario was fixed; no flashing advice is provided.

October 15 source; half an hour is secondhand timing, not independently verified.
