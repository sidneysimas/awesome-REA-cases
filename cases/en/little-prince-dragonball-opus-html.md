# From a Little Prince game to Dragon Ball-themed HTML 3D

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: Needs verification

梭哈.AI (@SUOHA_AI) reports having no game-development or 3D-modeling experience, using Opus 5.5 to study a Little Prince-themed AI 3D game and then making a Dragon Ball-themed 3D story game. The author says the model helped investigate and reconstruct scenes, story and interactions, claiming five hours and five yuan, providing an HTML download and describing the source as open. Rereading the original confirmed the account and demonstration video's existence. This collection did not obtain or run the HTML and cannot confirm source contents, cost, frame rate or functional equivalence, nor infer binary decompilation. The original explicitly says no other tools were used, so the work is not attributed to REA.

Original post: October 15, 20:44; candidate snapshot: 78 likes, 11,649 views, 7 reposts. Five hours/five yuan are author claims, not independently checked.
