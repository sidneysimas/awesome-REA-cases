# Ray-Ban Meta race coach: unverified REA camera access and Claude interpretation at Spa

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-28
Stage: Needs verification

At 22:45:37 Beijing time on October 28, 2026, a post describes a race coach combining Ray-Ban Meta glasses, REA and Claude Opus 5.5. REA allegedly studies the glasses to expose a 30fps camera feed to an agent; the model interprets each frame and supplies a racing line, braking distances, apex markers and spoken coaching. Spa's Eau Rouge, Kemmel and Les Combes are the setting; 240 and 262km/h are narrative video figures.

The author claims speed, gear, lap delta and g-force come from the dashboard and the trajectory is reconstructed from video. Video, telemetry, exact hardware, latency, protocol code and display path were not checked. Ordinary Ray-Ban Meta glasses are not thereby proven to have the described lens display, and video overlays are not real-time glasses functionality or driving-safety validation. This is a concrete unverified application claim, separate from chess assistance and an Android replacement client, not a recommendation to rely on it in a car.
