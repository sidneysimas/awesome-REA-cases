# LG OLED pause ads: an idea to modify firmware with Codex

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-18
Stage: Needs verification

At 04:32 Beijing time on October 18, @mxcondor says their LG OLED started showing ads during content pauses and as a screensaver the previous day. Blocking its ad servers reportedly also stops apps working. They propose two alternatives: use an Apple TV, or have Codex modify the TV firmware. This records a concrete idea to remove pause ads while retaining app functionality, not a completed firmware modification. The post supplies no TV model, firmware version, particular service shared by ads and apps, flashable image or build tests. It does not establish that Codex inspected the device, bypassed signing or safely removed ads. This is also separate from the existing LG Plex client and native Claude Code-on-LG projects; different authors' TV experiences are not combined into one successful modification.

Idea; candidate snapshot: 1 like, 43 views, not evidence of an implemented firmware change.
