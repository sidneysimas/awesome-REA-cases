# Android CarPlay AI box: a proposal for four Claude terminals on a parked car's display

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Related project

A reply at 21:16 Beijing time on October 19 supplies a complete proposal prompt. With an owned Ottocast, Carlinkit Tbox or similar Android CarPlay AI box, first explain the boundary between iPhone rendering and the head unit's display role, then show four laptop Claude Code processes in a tmux grid through a password-protected local ttyd webpage in the box's browser. The purpose is viewing and switching agents from the dashboard. The planned setup uses ADB, a shared hotspot and on-screen authorization, not iPhone reversing or jailbreaking.

The prompt requires confirming an Android box exists or stopping, backing up settings and saving undo commands. It prohibits touching CarPlay services, navigation, the reversing camera, CAN bus or safety apps, and requires parked operation without rooting or flashing. These are design constraints, not passed safety tests. There is no tested box model, source, recording or execution log. Four displayed agents are an acceptance goal, not an achieved result, and REA is not explicit. The technical premise differs from the BMW iDrive arbitrary-app promotion and does not substantiate its five-minute allowlist-bypass claim.
