# A Destiny community rebuild: reported Claude decompilation and in-engine assets

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

An earlier reply defending a community project says Claude decompiled d2 code downloadable on Steam and assets were recreated in-engine. The October 15, 01:26 Beijing-time Bungie/Destiny root post and WoTM raid discussion were read only to identify the background. The reply's simultaneous claims of Claude use and '0 generative ai' apparently distinguish decompilation assistance from generated assets, not absence of AI or a legal conclusion. At 14:38 on October 16, @isinternets says their own project also used AI and involved months of reverse engineering, questioning someone else's claim of completion in 24 hours. Reading their 14:34 parent thread identifies a dispute over rebuilding Destiny in Godot: objections concern the bubble-authority protocol, mission hosting, Havok 2012 collision and Umbra 3, suggesting a launcher or engine meshing instead. These are a participant's technical objections, not this collection establishing impossibility. Their own project cannot simply be equated with the one previously defended.

At 09:14 Beijing time on October 19, Envy (@WeLoveYouEnvy), discussing D1 maps moved to D2, says developers are using AI to demonstrate that content migration is possible and relays that another community developer used AI-assisted reverse engineering to port Wrath. Reading the 05:22 root and 08:48 reply confirms the map context; those earlier posts explain the candidate and are not new posts in this window. Envy supplies no developer identity, project link, source or tests. Completion is a secondhand account, and their criticism that Bungie has not ported it over the years is not technical verification. The new account cannot be equated with the earlier Claude/Godot implementation or treated as its independent reproduction.

No checkable project identity, code or runtime tests were obtained, so the entry retains a controversy around the same target without confirming a complete port, playability or a 24-hour achievement. REA use is unconfirmed.
