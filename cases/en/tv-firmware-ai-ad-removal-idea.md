# TV firmware: an AI ad-removal idea and two implementation accounts

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: Needs verification

On October 16, @j_larrieux replied to @DCinvestor's question about AI-made TV firmware that does not spy on its user, proposing decompiling current firmware, removing ads, recompiling and flashing it back with thorough AI-requested tests. That suggestion is an idea, not a completed repair. No brand, model, firmware, build or signing method, or ad/telemetry tests are supplied; safe flashing and overcoming signing restrictions cannot be assumed.

At 22:16 Beijing time that day, another respondent says they used Claude to investigate their TV firmware and disable tracking and advertising, also recommending AdGuard to block outbound traffic. This is the participant's own account without firmware patches, before-and-after traffic comparisons or code. Their generalization that most TV microphones listen to everything is unsupported and is not adopted here. At 22:56, @meta_syntactic replies to the same parent post that Codex had investigated existing firmware to remove unwanted parts, but they lost interest and got little beyond reverse engineering. Reading the parent confirms the TV privacy context, but not a model or modules to remove. These are different participants, not the same project; the first person's completion claim does not apply to the second. They are grouped under the existing topic to compare a proposal, claimed changes and a stalled investigation. None was run or privacy-tested by this collection.

October 16 original idea's candidate snapshot: 4 likes, 143 views; two later personal accounts have separate sources, neither with flashing or privacy acceptance tests.
