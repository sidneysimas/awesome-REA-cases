# GTA 6: predicting half the game reverse-engineered within three months of an executable dump

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-18
Stage: Needs verification

At 14:59 Beijing time on October 18, a poster bets that once someone dumps GTA 6's executable, AI agents will reverse-engineer about 50% of the game within three months. They also speculate that the disc will require new PS5 firmware blocking current jailbreaks, so obtaining the executable may take time first. The concrete target is game-binary research, but no acquired sample or started agent is reported.

The post does not define 50% in terms of functions, bytes, systems or gameplay coverage. Three months is a prediction, not measured effort or speed. Console firmware behavior is likewise unverified speculation. No version, model, REA involvement, repository or tests are supplied. This is a target-specific predictive idea, not evidence GTA 6 has been reversed or leaked, or the PS5 compromised.
