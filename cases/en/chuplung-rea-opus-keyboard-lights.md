# Natural-language keyboard lighting: chuplung reports replacing a large app with REA, Opus and Python

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-22
Stage: Needs verification

At 08:18 Beijing time on October 22, 2026, chuplung (@choopyplug1) says REA and Opus 5.5 replaced a 512 MB keyboard-lighting application with 15 lines of Python. The concrete use is an AI agent accepting a natural-language request such as a warm, dim evening mode and changing keyboard lighting. The post says the whole desk turns amber and includes a video.

The 512 MB, 15 lines and approximately 30 seconds are the author's figures, not measurements by this collection. It is unclear whether 30 seconds means research, implementation or a single operation. Original text and video-attachment information were read, but the lighting behavior was not independently watched and verified. No keyboard make/model, protocol, script, permission requirements or compatibility range are supplied. Coverage of other settings in the original app is unknown. This does not establish that drivers are unnecessary, that other devices work or that all desk devices are controlled. It is not merged with other named keyboard-protocol projects.
