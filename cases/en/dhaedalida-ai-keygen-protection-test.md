At 04:51 Beijing time on October 18, software-protector vendor @dhaedalida reports an AI reverse-engineering test on an application it made, amid discussion of AI recreating commercial software. It says AI generated a working keygen for the unprotected version in 3 minutes. After Dhaedalida protection was applied, the model ran for over 4 hours without producing a working keygen, while a simpler algorithm was still recovered despite protection. The test therefore concerns both licensing design and code protection, not a conclusion that protection inevitably defeats AI.
