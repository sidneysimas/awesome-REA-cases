# A 3D home dashboard: Clinton Mills uses AI to connect hard-to-integrate products

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 04:10 Beijing time on October 19, @Clintonm9 (Clinton Mills) reports using AI over two days to build a 3D model of both house floors and the exterior, plus a dashboard connecting home systems. Reverse-engineering products that resist integration was reportedly the hardest part. This is more than generating a house image, but not a claim to have broken all home-device firmware.

His 04:10:18 reply lists iCloud family location, Control4, Clare, Amazon Alexa, SmartHQ, myQ, iAquaLink, Somfy shades, Samsung TVs and Bryant HVAC as connected, with dinner, chores, calendars and home controls together. This supplementary reply defines scope, not which integrations use public APIs, which were reversed or which are read-only. Each is not treated as an independent protocol breakthrough.

No model, REA usage, source, specific protocol or control tests are supplied. Two days and successful connections remain attributed to the author; no devices or family data were independently inspected.
