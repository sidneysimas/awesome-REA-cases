# iOS on Android hardware: a porting idea and unverified HACKIOS/Redmi claims

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 22:24 Beijing time on October 16, the poster conditionally proposes that if AI reverse-engineered iOS and installed it on Android phones, it might run more smoothly than on Apple hardware, then extrapolates that software progress could reduce the value of hardware upgrades. The concrete target is an operating system port across hardware, but the source reports no personal experiment, kernel, drivers, boot chain, device model or implementation plan. What is collected is an explicit exploration idea, not a successful iOS port. Smoothness, deeper understanding than Apple and an end to meaningful phone hardware upgrades are untested inferences.

Earlier review revisited the October 16 HACKIOS promotion at 22:59 and Redmi post at 23:03, and read an account at 06:22 on October 17. These add background on the same functional target, not confirmation that the posts and original idea describe one project. HACKIOS claims a port to any phone and the Redmi post claims operation, but the conversation supplies no device model, boot record, kernel/driver code or original-author tests, only a group link, praise and skepticism. Comments about a Linux kernel with iOS userland or an Android theme are speculation, not implementation explanations. A prediction that Apple will issue a statement is promotional speculation too. Neither REA nor any model is confirmed to have implemented a full OS port. Genuine iOS operation, camera/cellular support, app installation and performance remain unverified.

In a new October 17 reply at 16:07, another reader says running 64-bit iOS apps on Android requires extensive reverse engineering even with AI help, and that they are trying it. They require a credible third party before believing the full-iOS rumor. No app name, code, logs or success is supplied, and the specific use of AI cannot be confirmed. App compatibility must not be conflated with an OS port, nor is this established as the HACKIOS project. The in-progress account is retained without promoting it to a porting result; REA involvement remains unconfirmed.
