# Tekken Tag Tournament 2: Shihab's AI-assisted PC port moves toward stability maintenance

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-24
Stage: In progress

At 08:23:19 Beijing time on October 24, 2026, Shihab Hussain (@shihab_7ussain) explains how his Tekken port relates to Project BlueSpark. He started about two months earlier, paused, resumed this month and learned about BlueSpark only several days after his first release. His stated motivation was fun and seeing how far AI could go at reverse engineering and building the features he wanted. He calls the port open source, invites reuse and plans primarily stability maintenance rather than many new features. BlueSpark belongs to another team; their work and results must not be combined. AI involvement is explicit, but the post gives no model, REA invocation, human/AI division of work or reconstruction method.

To identify the target, this review also read the same author's v0.1.7 announcement at 04:50:34 Beijing time on October 24, before this window's 08:00 start, not a new candidate. It identifies a Tekken Tag Tournament 2 PC port and lists rollback netcode, input-delay/rollback settings, rankings and leaderboards, teams, replays and ghosts, self-hosted Windows/Linux community servers, Steam friend invitations and two local players online. Graphics claims include install-time shader preparation, mipmaps, sharpening, FXAA, texture filtering, VSYNC and low-resolution options. Users must supply an Xbox 360 disc and Title Update 3, and both online players need v0.1.7. These are release claims: no source code, installer or acceptance logs were obtained, so stutter elimination, reliable networking and feature completeness are not independently tested conclusions.

This collection downloaded no game and ran no port. Open-source availability is the author's statement, without repository-license verification. Confirming AI involvement does not mean all code was AI-generated or that original source was recovered. This is an in-progress AI-assisted game-preservation and PC-interoperability project.

Candidate: October 24, 2026, 08:23:19; supplemental release: 04:50:34, both Beijing time. The latter identifies the target/version and is not a new candidate.
Open-source availability, features and performance are author claims. The model, AI division of work and source code were not verified; the port was not independently run.
