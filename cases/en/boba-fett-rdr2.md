# Boba Fett in Red Dead Redemption 2

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-12

Chris and Angaisb_ wanted to bring Boba Fett from Star Wars: Battlefront II into Red Dead Redemption 2 (RDR2). Existing export tools did not satisfy their requirements of no registration and no payment. The author says Claude analyzed the file format and wrote an exporter, adapted the model to Arthur's skeleton and added a jetpack, weapons and voice lines. The post shows flying and attacks. This is asset-format conversion and modding, not complete decompilation of both games.
