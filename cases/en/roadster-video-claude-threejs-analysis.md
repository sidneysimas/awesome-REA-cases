# Roadster video: a Claude-assisted three.js visual teardown experiment

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-17
Stage: Analysis test

At 06:38 Beijing time on October 17, @Flinzu says reference frames from a Tesla Roadster demonstration were given to Claude Code to study appearance and motion without blueprints, then separate and label parts in three.js and produce a 20-second exploded-view animation. The post describes first establishing wheelbase and roofline, tying claims to frames and using deterministic frame rendering. The author says an initial box-like model looked like a toy and improved only after proportions were corrected, while six of fourteen parts stayed unknown because they were not visible.

This is an author report of image-based shape inference and visualization, not vehicle-software inspection or a physical teardown. Lift, flips, thrust and '46 million views' come from the post's narrative about the video, not this collection's verified vehicle performance, structure, manufacturability or engagement snapshot. REA is an analogy to app analysis, not a reported vehicle tool. The original video's authenticity, model code and render output were not checked, and speculation under hidden panels is not treated as mechanical fact.
