# Pokémon HeartGold/SoulSilver: a secondhand multi-agent decompilation and cost report

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: Needs verification

A reply says multiple AI agents separately decompiled chunks of Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver and assembled them, finishing in one month with roughly 71 billion tokens. It explicitly distinguishes these from local-model tokens, naming Claude/OpenAI APIs, and says the work was subsidized. Around $56,000 is described as a hypothetical cost estimated from API usage on those platforms, not an actual bill. No developer identity, repository, matching percentage, tests or cost worksheet is supplied. Game scope, completion, time and tokens remain secondhand claims; they do not establish clean-room work, absence of existing public material or a completed playable port. Another short item in this review only repeats HeartGold, a month and the list-price estimate, adding no substantive evidence. REA use is unconfirmed.

October 16 candidate snapshot: 1 like, 22 views; roughly 71 billion tokens, one month and $56,000 are not independently verified.
