# Harvest Moon: Back to Nature: a REA-assisted PS1-to-PC model-comparison account

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-20
Stage: Needs verification

At 07:49 Beijing time on October 20, a Portuguese-speaking poster says they gave Claude Opus 5.5 Max and GPT 6.1 Sol Max the same prompt to port the PS1 game Harvest Moon: Back to Nature natively to PC with keyboard and Xbox-controller support, using REA. The game involves farming, crops and village life; the reported purpose is cross-platform play and input adaptation.

The author claims Claude completed it in five hours with no problems from start to finish, whereas GPT was still trying at posting time after approximately four weekly quotas, including three previously available resets, and had created 7,004 files without functional output. These are the author's descriptions of two jobs, not a standardized benchmark or confirmation of complete playthrough functionality.

No original prompt, source, game version, completion scope, running video, tool-call trace or billing log is supplied. Equal context and environments cannot be confirmed, and weekly quotas cannot be converted to dollar costs. Model names and results remain attributed to the post. This collection ran no game, recompilation or independent reproduction and does not infer general model superiority.
