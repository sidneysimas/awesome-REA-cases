Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA

Stage: Needs verification

At 22:31 Beijing time on October 18, a reader asks whether REA could investigate iOS and Android so users can sideload apps again. The purpose is freedom to install applications on mobile systems, not rebuilding a particular app or reporting an existing jailbreak.
