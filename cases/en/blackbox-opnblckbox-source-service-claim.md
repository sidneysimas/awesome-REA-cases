# Blackbox/opnblckbox: claims and criticism of an agent-swarm source-reconstruction service

Category: Applications and clients · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

The older deferred candidate is @Larpcheckbot's October 19 reply at 15:33 Beijing time. It rates @opnblckbox's reverse-engineer-anything claim LIKELY LARP (24/100), saying only a job form and token exist and no job has returned source. An earlier parent identifies Blackbox. Its account's 11:56 post advertises giving a paid bot, app, game or locked binary to a swarm of agents to receive working source, with payment only when it runs, using the $BLACKBOX tag.

The inputs and reconstruction deliverable are concrete enough to retain an unverified project. The promotion supplies no sample, toolchain, code, build or verifiable customer order. The bot likewise supplies no itemized checks or delivery database in the readable text, so its claim of no deliveries and 24/100 score are not this collection's independent findings. Neither service success, fraud nor fund safety is established, and morluto/rea use is not inferred.

Reviewing the older October 19 candidate at 12:23 on October 27 identifies its author as @R0setiger, directly replying to @opnblckbox's official promotion at 12:07 that day. The previously unidentified surface/Tor account can therefore be attributed as a secondhand Blackbox description rather than left identity-deferred. It describes binaries up to 50 MB or a URL, package name or contract address, ETH on the surface and XMR/ZEC over Tor, payment only on success at 0.025 ETH (about $60 in the post), no account, a one-hour retrieval code and deletion of inputs and results after delivery. The official parent explicitly promotes Tor, Monero and anonymity but does not confirm every price, size or deletion detail in its text. Those remain R0setiger's account, not an actual order or privacy audit. This is backlog review with earlier parent context, not new posts from October 27's 00:00–04:00 window. No Tor service was accessed, file submitted, payment made or target code executed.
