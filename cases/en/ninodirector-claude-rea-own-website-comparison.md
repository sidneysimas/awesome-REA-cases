# Rebuilding an owned website: Nino reports plain Claude finishing first while REA still refined mobile

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-26
Stage: Analysis test

At 22:31:22-23 Beijing time on October 26, 2026, Nino | Máquina IA (@ninodirector) posted three reports of giving two Claude sessions the same instruction to reconstruct his own website, one using REA and one without added tools. He says plain Claude finished first in just over eight minutes, including an explanation and comparison with the original at 14 scroll positions. The REA session was still polishing mobile when its time expired. The third post says both reconstructions looked very close to the original side by side, concluding that plain Claude suffices for an ordinary website while REA is better suited to apps or binaries without source. Supplementary reading of the public thread confirmed attribution and the three posts' relationship; subsequent La Fábrica course promotion is not an experimental result.

The concrete negative boundary is that REA did not finish first on this website task, not that its output entirely failed or was unusable.
