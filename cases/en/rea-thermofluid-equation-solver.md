# A thermofluid equation solver: ongoing REA/Opus work

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · REA
Record date: 2026-10-15
Stage: In progress

@iamsupersocks reports using Opus 5.5 since Monday on two reverse-engineering projects: an Age of Empires-style mobile/web RTS and an equation solver for thermal engineering, fluid mechanics and mechanics. The author says both are nearly complete, using T3 Code, Opus 5.5 and REA, advancing piece by piece, regularly challenging the model and testing for drift; an open beta is planned for the RTS. The solver targets old Excel and legacy-software workflows. A simulation is claimed to have fallen from five hours to five minutes, but without the original software name, equations, input scale, accuracy or same-machine comparison, this is not a measured 60× speedup. Both uses are explained in one development report, with this entry primarily about the solver; the RTS is not called a decompilation of Age of Empires. Neither was independently run.

Candidate snapshot: 12 likes | 866 views; performance and near-completion are author claims.
