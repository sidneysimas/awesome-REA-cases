# Makara game maker: an idea for scoped analysis of authorized inputs

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-18
Stage: Needs verification

At 01:20 and 01:31 Beijing time on October 18, 2026, @skyshark88 discusses a dedicated Makara game-creation program, not a released implementation. Users would supply descriptions, example files and assets they have rights to use. AI would extract sprites and summarize simple mechanics and structure only within the project's workspace, create a new game and package an Android APK. Keeping arbitrary-APK analysis out of the main Shell and general app-development layer is an explicit permission boundary.

The follow-up proposes a playable slice of about one minute, with character, color, animation, UI and genre-template changes followed by fast preview updates. The author warns that freely remixing famous characters and commercial assets creates copyright risks and recommends original, clearly licensed or user-generated assets. These are product-design suggestions, without a repository, installable app, sandbox-security tests or actual reversing target. A working directory is not described as proven strong isolation, and no legal guarantee is made.

Sources: October 18, 2026, 01:20 and 01:31 Beijing time; an idea without implementation or sandbox tests.
