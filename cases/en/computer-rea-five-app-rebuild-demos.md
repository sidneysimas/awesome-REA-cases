# Computer integrates REA: five app-feature rebuilding demos including Figma

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-17
Stage: Needs verification

At 23:42 Beijing time on October 16, Computer's official @AskPerplexity account announced @morluto's REA as a skill inside Computer, claiming it can investigate apps without source and build working versions. The candidate directly presents Figma-like layers, auto-layout, sign-in, cross-device sync and multiplayer editing. Supplementary reading of the same author's 23:43 replies adds LibreOffice Calc cells, formulas and updating charts; Photopea layers, masks, color adjustments and editable text; Harvey contract search, version comparison, rule-based clause flags and source citations; and Audacity track trimming/mixing, volume, fades and WAV export. These are five demos in one product announcement, not five independent reproductions.

The account says the demos are temporarily available and /reverse-engineer tasks are free through October 31, up to 10,000 credits per user. These are contemporaneous promotional terms, not permanent free access. The text and demo links did not supply analysis logs, source, original-app comparisons or persistent-service guarantees obtained by this collection. Multiplayer behavior, real-file compatibility, formula coverage and contract-analysis correctness were not verified. An official announcement establishes the platform's feature claims, not complete cloning or safe production deployment. This collection did not run demos, register for the service or process real contracts or accounts.

At 11:45 on October 17, a reader says they logged into the Figma-like Assemblage and found only some core capabilities, with many features missing. This is a user's observation account, not a complete feature matrix or this collection's independent trial. Their suggestion that the developers intentionally stopped for fear of litigation is unsupported and is not adopted, nor does it establish that an individual could fully clone the original.
