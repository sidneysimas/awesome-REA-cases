# Ray-Ban Meta chess assistant: 0xProbability reports reversed glasses with AI board reading and move display

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-28
Stage: Needs verification

Historical sources at 16:37 and 19:24 Beijing time on October 26, 2026 describe reversed Ray-Ban Meta glasses reading chessboards with AI and suggesting moves; the first credits @0xProbabillity. Supplementary lookup finds the October 24, 18:42 original: 0xProbability says he reversed his own glasses so a supposedly 3000+ Elo AI reads the board and displays moves in AR, with video. That earlier post establishes attribution, not a new October 28 experiment. The target is glasses as a chess assistant, distinct from the Android app forwarding Hey Meta to Claude.

At 05:08:57 on October 28, Codez (@0xCodez) gives a secondhand account of Meta Ray-Bans 3 with Opus 5.5 and Jev, using a WhatsApp video call from glasses to Mac for frames: about one per second while waiting for the opponent, one every three seconds on the wearer's turn. Jev filters obscured/unchanged frames, Opus reads the board and checks legal moves, Stockfish thinks for half a second, and Mac speech returns through the call. Claimed features include a status/confidence/history panel, PGN saves, Keychain credentials and OBS video arrows. Lost state permits undo/manual input and Stockfish restarts once after a crash. The retelling does not directly credit 0xProbability or provide a repository; identity with the original AR implementation is unconfirmed.

At 11:09:04 on October 28, m (@mm_dyh) replies directly to Codez that no reversing is visible: all work occurs on a Mac over a standard WhatsApp call. They suggest an iPhone could process frames, perform vision and run Stockfish locally without paid cloud models. Thread lookup confirms the reply concerns the Codez workflow above. This is a concrete technical criticism and alternative idea, not a device test, implemented iPhone solution or independent disproof; it cannot rule out undisclosed protocol research.

A Mac video pipeline and speech/video overlay do not establish an on-glasses engine, modified firmware or native AR. AI board reading is distinct from AI-assisted protocol reversing, which remains unknown. Open source, third-generation hardware, models and features are not confirmed by source. Recognition accuracy, end-to-end latency, win rates and device tests are absent; videos were not verified. 3000+ Elo, 20,000 hours and zero seconds are not project competitive measurements. Half a second is engine-thinking time, not whole-pipeline latency. No competitive cheating instructions are provided, glasses operated or code executed here. REA is unknown and status remains unverified.
