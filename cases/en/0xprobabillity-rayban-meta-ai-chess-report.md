# Ray-Ban Meta chess assistant: 0xProbability reports reversed glasses with AI board reading and move display

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-28
Stage: Needs verification

Historical sources at 16:37 and 19:24 Beijing time on October 26, 2026 describe reversed Ray-Ban Meta glasses reading chessboards with AI and suggesting moves. The first credits @0xProbabillity. Supplementary lookup found the October 24, 18:42 original: 0xProbability says he reversed his own glasses so a supposedly 3000+ Elo AI reads the board and displays the best move in AR, with a video. That earlier post establishes attribution, not a new October 28 experiment. The target is glasses connected as a chess assistant, distinct from the Android app forwarding Hey Meta to Claude.

At 05:08:57 on October 28, Codez (@0xCodez) gives a more detailed secondhand workflow, claiming Meta Ray-Bans 3 with Opus 5.5 and Jev. A WhatsApp video call from glasses to Mac supplies frames, about one per second while waiting for the opponent and one every three seconds during the wearer's turn. Jev filters obscured or unchanged frames; Opus reads the board and checks legal moves; Stockfish thinks for half a second; Mac speech returns through the call. The account also claims a status/confidence/history panel, PGN saves, Keychain credentials and video arrows for OBS. Lost board state permits undo/manual input, and Stockfish restarts once after a crash.

The new retelling does not directly credit 0xProbability or supply a repository. It is retained as an additional implementation claim about this type of glasses chess assistant, not proof of exactly the same implementation as the original AR showcase. A Mac video pipeline and speech/video overlay do not establish an on-glasses engine, rewritten firmware or native AR display. AI clearly reads boards; AI-assisted protocol reversing remains unknown. Open source, third-generation hardware, models and features remain secondhand. Source, recognition accuracy, end-to-end latency, win rates and device tests were not obtained; videos were not verified. 3000+ Elo, 20,000 hours and zero seconds are not project competitive measurements. Half a second is claimed engine thinking time, not whole-pipeline latency. No competitive cheating instructions are provided, glasses operated or code executed here. REA is unknown and status remains unverified.

The October 24 original establishes attribution and target, not a newly collected October 28 experiment.
AI board reading, communication reversing, AR, speech and 3000+ Elo are distinct claims; the new pipeline is not proven to be the same implementation as the original AR demonstration.
