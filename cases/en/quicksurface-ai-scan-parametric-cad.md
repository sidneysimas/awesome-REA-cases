# QUICKSURFACE AI: announced scan-to-parametric-CAD automation

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-15
Stage: Related project

QUICKSURFACE's promotional announcement in this window says QUICKSURFACE AI will launch on November 17, 2026, included in Pro 2027, aiming to turn 3D scans into editable parametric CAD models in one click. Here reverse engineering means recovering engineering geometry from physical measurements, not decompiling commercial CAD software; it is separately identified as a related project in the broader sense of AI-assisted reverse engineering. The evidence is only a future-product announcement, without models, error measurements, scan samples or acceptance of an actual demonstration. The planned date is not a completed release and one-click results are not endorsed.

Future release date in the announcement is a product plan; record date remains October 15.
