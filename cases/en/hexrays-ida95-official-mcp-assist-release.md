# IDA 9.5: Hex-Rays announces MCP/Assist and recursive call-graph decompilation

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work

At 03:56:49 Beijing time on October 27, 2026, Hex-Rays SA (@HexRaysSA) announces IDA 9.5. An original-post lookup confirms the official attribution and complete text: new Android Dalvik, Infineon TriCore and Qualcomm Hexagon decompilers; IDA MCP and Assist for AI-driven reversing; recursive decompilation across call graphs; and IDA Home's cloud decompilers increasing from 2 to 11 at an unchanged price, with downloads available to active license holders.
