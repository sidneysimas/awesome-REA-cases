# Super Mario Sunshine: reported AI-assisted PC port

Category: Games and preservation · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-12
Stage: Needs verification

Adil shared a native PC port of Super Mario Sunshine, saying it was playable after AI-agent decompilation and mentioning HD textures, cross-platform support and 120 FPS. A public sms-pc-port project does offer a port based on matching decompilation, with supported playable platforms and optional 120 FPS listed in its README, and requires the user's own game image. However, this review could not confirm that the circulating post's short link points to that repository, and the project README does not establish AI's specific contribution. The report therefore remains unverified and the whole port is not credited to AI.
