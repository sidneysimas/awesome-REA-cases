# Point Smart: Conra reports stalled terminal reversing and an AI/official-SmartApps SDK pivot

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-29
Stage: In progress

At 10:10:21 Beijing time on October 29, 2026, Conra (@Conra22a) reports continuing research on a Mercado Pago Point Smart payment terminal, mainly using Astra and DeepSeek v4 after repeated safety alerts from Anthropic models. He distinguishes an Android-running Cortex-A53 application processor, AP, from an ARMv7-M secure processor, SP, holding keys and handling EMV payments, and finds separate research difficult. Possible key erasure and device lockout on anti-tamper activation are his risk explanation, not hardware findings by this collection.

The author explicitly lacks hardware-opening expertise and does not want to damage or lock the terminal. He calls the reversing experience 'unsuccessful so far' while planning continued research on the intact device. A second route on the same device uses AI and skills based on REA's repository to develop SmartApps. In his description, the Mercado Pago SDK supports private merchant apps distributed to terminals by serial number, using native Android, Flutter or Ionic. He wants to demonstrate useful products for shops, restaurants and cafes and announces a forthcoming tutorial.

This is not a secure-processor compromise, payment-key recovery or EMV bypass. SDK application development is not proof of reversing success. Lookup confirms author and full text; device revision, official SDK documentation, code, installation and payment tests were not checked. REA attribution concerns the reported derived skills only. No target code was run, device opened or payment performed here.

Candidate snapshot: 105 likes, 6,191 views, 5 reposts; lookup-time growth is not substituted.
Two routes on one device. Research continues, reversing has failed so far, the tutorial is planned and official-SDK capabilities were not independently verified.
