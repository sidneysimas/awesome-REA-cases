Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA

Stage: Needs verification

At 22:50:08 Beijing time on October 23, 2026, KutahyAli describes a clinic's periapical dental imaging-plate scanner and vendor software: images reportedly took about 70–75 seconds to appear, with 2.5 GB RAM use. Neither manufacturer nor device model is named; a known brand must not be invented.

Two immediate same-author replies explain connecting Opus 5.5 to the computer over SSH from a personal laptop, supplying REA tools and free DeepSeek agents through OpenCode. The author reports four hours of work to write a Rust replacement excluding unnecessary bulky features, with images now appearing in about 30 seconds and RAM around 40 MB.
