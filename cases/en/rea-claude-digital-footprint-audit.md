# Digital-footprint cleanup: REA investigates trackers and Claude assists deletion requests

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-20
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:06 Beijing time on October 20, an author reports spending seven hours the previous week with Claude Opus 5.5 and REA cleaning their digital footprint. They search their own information, list targets, request opt-outs from Spokeo, BeenVerified, Whitepages and MyLife, use REA to identify trackers and third parties in old sites/apps, and ask Claude for account-deletion steps and CCPA/GDPR requests. They report removing 53 name/phone/address records, closing nine old accounts and finding 14 companies they had never registered with holding their data.
