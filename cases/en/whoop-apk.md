# WHOOP Android app investigation

Category: Applications and clients · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-12
Stage: Needs verification

WHOOP provides health and fitness tracking devices and associated services. The original record is an APK discussion shared by uwwgo; it does not establish a publicly usable alternative client. An October 15 candidate separately recounts a developer canceling a subscription after 18 months and creating Zhoop: it reads the band directly over Bluetooth, stores data locally on an iPhone and simplifies the tool to calorie management for personal needs. The account says Zhoop derives from NOOP, whose earlier protocol research used Claude Code. This is a new use-case lead for the same target, insufficient to identify the original APK author as Zhoop's developer or establish recovery of all WHOOP metrics or official algorithms. This collection did not additionally inspect either project's source or reproduce communications; the unverified status and original source are retained.
