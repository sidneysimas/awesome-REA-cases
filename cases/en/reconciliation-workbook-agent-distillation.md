# Excel reconciliation workbooks: infer a procedure from an accepted artifact for agents

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work

On October 16, @burkov described research on replication-driven knowledge distillation. Enterprise reconciliation logic is scattered across spreadsheets, databases, documentation, email and chat, with missing or conflicting evidence. An agent uses a validated artifact such as a signed-off Excel reconciliation workbook to infer a procedure and distill reusable structured knowledge, then is evaluated on the same task for a new time period and detailed process questions. The target is business calculations and operating rules, not decompiling Excel or recovering all enterprise knowledge.
