# Touhou TH04: ring-bullet angles

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-12

TH04 is Touhou Gensokyo, a bullet-hell shooter in the Touhou Project series originally running in a PC-98 DOS environment. The contributor investigated how enemies fire a ring of bullets: recovering angular spacing, the offset for aiming at the player, template rotation and the logic that writes the final angles, with C++ and explanatory diagrams. REA's instruction checks refer to compilation-match records from a historical reconstruction project; this was not a new reconstruction of the entire game.
