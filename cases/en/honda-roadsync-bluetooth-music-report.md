# Honda RoadSync: Claude-assisted scooter Bluetooth research for other music apps

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-18
Stage: Needs verification

At 22:52 Beijing time on October 17, 2026, Busy K (@Busy__K) says Honda's iPhone RoadSync app only lets him play Apple Music, so he had Claude reverse-engineer the scooter's Bluetooth and build a personal app. He reports that the vehicle's buttons now control Spotify, YouTube Music and podcasts. The concrete purpose is reusing existing vehicle controls with other players, not replacing vehicle firmware or controlling driving systems. The RoadSync limitation is the author's experience, not an official feature conclusion across regions, versions or models.

Reading the original confirms the author and two image attachments, but no vehicle model, protocol fields, source code, pairing steps or player-compatibility record is published. The post does not attribute REA use, and 'anything' is not evidence that every app was tested. Bluetooth reversing and successful control remain author reports; this collection did not access a vehicle or independently test them.
