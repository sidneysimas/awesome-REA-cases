# Sonic physics in Elden Ring: an unverified REA mod report

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-15
Stage: Needs verification

The poster says REA and Opus 5.5 were used to study character movement in the 1991 Sonic binary and integrate a spin dash, ring collection and speed display into Elden Ring. They list maximum speed $0600, jump $0680 and gravity $0038, claim six numbers capture the key feel, and report about 40 minutes. The post recommends locating original movement functions, finding the target game's player-movement entry point and rebuilding the controller. No replayable mod, constant-location records or test logs are supplied, so the numbers, timing and cross-engine effects are unconfirmed. 'No code copied' is also only the poster's claim. This is a specific but unverified mod report, not a general capability for arbitrary games.
