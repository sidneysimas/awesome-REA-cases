Observations become an operator-signed parity checklist with inputs, expected outputs and checks. Completion requires every must-have to pass two consecutive reviews. Two stalled iterations, exhausted budget, protected material or a required vendor backend trigger a stop and question; untested items cannot pass. Same-input output comparisons, format round trips and screen comparisons are prescribed methods, not tests this collection observed being executed.

Learning means staging checklist patterns, review techniques and fix recipes for operator approval before writing them back to the skill, not updating model weights.
