# vartekxx's drone-tracking proposal: REA traces camera/control paths and Opus organizes phone-side control

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-27
Stage: Needs verification

At 04:36:19 Beijing time on October 27, 2026, vartekxx (@vartekxx) says he reverse-engineered his drone with REA and Opus 5.5 for area-security tracking. The aim is phone-side vision and control. No-cloud and no-GPS claims remain attributed to him; they do not establish that the aircraft needs no navigation system or that all models are supported. Original-post lookup confirms attribution and text.

The steps first give Claude Code the exact model, firmware, companion app and sensors, then ask REA to inspect software and trace camera streams, telemetry and flight commands before connecting a supported control interface. They then propose camera-based person detection/tracking, obstacle mapping with supported depth or LiDAR sensors, speed/distance limits and pilot takeover. Simulation covers tracking, obstacles and connection loss before open-area testing with a consenting participant and a pilot ready to intervene. A final dashboard would show camera, state, map, speed, altitude and distance.

Although presented as an already adapted security drone, the text primarily supplies a build workflow without model, recovered protocol, source code, sensor configuration or simulation/flight records. This collection did not inspect the video or fly a drone, and does not confirm avoidance, target reacquisition or safety. Retained as unverified. The use resembles Codez's DJI official-SDK proposal, but this is another author's account, not the same implementation or a mutual reproduction.

Candidate snapshot: 17 likes, 725 views, 1 repost. Later engagement is not backfilled.
Simulation, safe distance and pilot takeover are proposed steps, not passed flight acceptance tests.
