# Visual Chess.com review: Tjay reports a Claude rebuild paired with local Qwen

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-25
Stage: Needs verification

At 09:12:32 Beijing time on October 25, 2026, @type_tjay said Claude had replaced a $100 Chess.com subscription. The author reports reverse-engineering its review engine and pairing the result with a four-bit-quantized Qwen 3.5 35B running locally on an M5 Pro Mac. As a visual learner who finds algebraic move notation difficult to interpret, the author gave the AI drawing tools to explain moves directly over the board.

The author also reports automatic synchronization of Chess.com games back to account creation, claims Mac, Windows and browser versions are available, and promises to open source it over the weekend. The post gives neither the billing period of the $100 subscription nor total development cost; this is not a verified savings figure. Which existing chess engines may be reused, equivalence to proprietary Chess.com scoring, and coverage of all historical games and platforms remain unconfirmed.
