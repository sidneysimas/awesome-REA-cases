# A robot vacuum: an idea for Claude to replace built-in cleaning decisions

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: Needs verification

Joe, LuckyJoe198x, first replies that he wants to reverse engineer his own robot vacuum and have Claude direct cleaning around his home instead of its built-in program. A subsequent standalone post expands the AI-supervisor idea: maximum power for dirty areas, more assertive entry into repeatedly missed corners, and using obstacle-climbing mechanical legs to descend a slope instead of rushing down it. Follow-up retrieval confirmed the same author for both posts, which are combined here. The target is replacing a specific device's cleaning decisions, not an implemented local cloud or remote-control client. The author supplies no brand, model, protocol, code or runtime result. Mechanical legs and similar capabilities are proposals, not confirmation that the device has them or can safely expose them to control. Actual reverse engineering is not reported and REA use is not explicit; this is included as a concrete AI-device-control idea.

October 16 candidate snapshots: initial reply 1 like, 12 views; follow-up 1 like, 87 views; neither reports implementation.
