# Tuya AC: local Mac controls exposed to Claude over MCP

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-15
Stage: Needs verification

Frustrated by a remote kept beside the bed that required leaving the desk during work, the author says they studied a Tuya-based air conditioner, obtained their own local key, built a Mac menu-bar Wi-Fi controller that avoids the cloud, and added an MCP service so Claude could accept requests such as setting 24 degrees. This explicitly connects a device to an AI tool; it does not mean Claude helped reverse-engineer the protocol. No model, code, packet captures or tests are public, and REA involvement is unconfirmed. It is separate from TCL air conditioners, Tuya lights and other authors' whole-home control records.

Concrete interoperability project; not reproduced; device keys not collected or published.
