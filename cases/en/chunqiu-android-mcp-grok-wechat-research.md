# Chunqiu's Android MCP framework: Grok Bot research on WeChat toward agent-operated phone apps

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work

At 01:42:24 Beijing time on October 23, 2026, Chunqiu (@chnqi05504825) says he built an Android phone MCP framework using AI and Android privilege-escalation issues. The stated goal is to let Grok Bot reverse engineer phone apps and delegate food orders, hotel and flight bookings, shopping price comparisons and coupon combination to an agent instead of opening each app manually.
