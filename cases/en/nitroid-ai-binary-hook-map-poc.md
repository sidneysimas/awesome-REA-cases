# Map/mission PoC: AI-assisted offsets and binary hooks, with an impractical content workflow

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-20
Stage: In progress

In an older deferred reply at 06:11 Beijing time on October 20, the author explains that this is not AI building the entire project in the usual sense. AI assists reverse engineering, offset identification and binary hooking, while individual components are constructed granularly. Map and mission creation is specifically involved, making this research into an existing game's binary and content-building workflow.

The author explicitly calls it a proof of concept and says the actual map/mission creation method is impractical. Previously retrieved discussion associated it with Nitroid but did not establish the game's identity. This entry does not guess a game, engine, tool or efficiency figure and does not present the PoC as a usable mod editor. The AI role and limits are concrete enough to change the earlier defer to an in-progress report. It is reassessment from the original October 20 04:00–08:00 window, not independently executed and without confirmed REA use.
