# Rust Duty: reconstructing gameplay from video, not decompilation

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: In progress

Rust Duty's author states the research question explicitly: can an LLM extract gameplay from video rather than decompile a game? The author provides animation and gameplay videos from MW2019, 2022, 2023 and what the post calls MW4, reporting a week using two OpenAI Dots, Claude and Grokbot to implement a Wgpu renderer, parse telemetry to improve FPS, and add default lighting and basic UI. Weapon and hand models are assets previously purchased for an Arma project; the author says they were not used for AI training. This is an ongoing reconstruction from visible behavior, not recovered original source or all gameplay restored. No performance figures, video-to-rule verification or independent execution are supplied.

October 15 source; tool and video names follow the original; asset authorization is author-reported.
