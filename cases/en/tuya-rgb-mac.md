# Tuya RGB lights from a Mac

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-12

Lucho bought RGB lights based on Tuya technology and did not want to depend on the manufacturer's mobile app every time. The author used Claude to help analyze Bluetooth data sent while an iPhone controlled the lights, identify the advertising format, sequence numbers and checksum rules, and then send control commands from a Mac script. The post includes a video of the lights being controlled. The target was the bulbs' communication protocol, addressing the restriction that lights the author owned could only be controlled through a designated app.
