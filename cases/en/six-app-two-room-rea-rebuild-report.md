# Six-app two-room reconstruction: a report of 299 claims and a negative-control judge rule

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-18
Stage: Needs verification

At 06:53 Beijing time on October 18, a poster reports nine nights using REA 6.5.0 and Opus 5.5 to study six apps they use. Room A records 299 claims labelled observed, derived or inferred, retains seven unknowns and produces six behavior-only SPEC.md files without code. Room B, on a second account, cannot see the original apps and builds from those specifications alone. The author stresses that REA is an MCP server, not an agent, and ships no clean room; they built the separation themselves.

The post proposes a judge rule: a deliberately broken build must fail all of the stated 412 tests, motivated by a Touhou reconstruction example where 0.0 reportedly passed in place of 128.0. Here 412 is the author's described rule and test scale, not inspected negative-control results or proof that all six apps passed. The author reports three sessions being rerouted to Opus 4.8 mid-run, no DRM or licensing-check work, a $187 token bill and cancellation of six subscriptions totalling $214 per month or $2,568 per year. These are reported figures, not independent confirmation of a uniform model setup, behavioral equivalence or savings.

No app names, specifications, claim evidence, test implementation or bills are supplied. This remains a concrete account of an isolation experiment, not reproduced replacement products; how the seven unknowns affect coverage is unexplained. Absence of Claude from a tool's reconstruction log does not imply Claude cannot use REA.

Candidate snapshot: 11 likes, 274 views; 299 claims, seven unknowns, 412 tests, $187 and subscription savings are unverified reported figures.
