# RINGFALL: a reported browser boss fight built from TH04 bullet-ring notes

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-16
Stage: Needs verification

mal0r1key describes a browser bullet-hell game called RINGFALL: REA drives Ghidra to inspect a PC-98 Touhou binary, records fixed and aimed ring-angle rules, and Opus 5.5 / Claude Code creates rings.ts, boss.ts and game.ts. The stated rules are phase + i·2π/n and atan2(player-boss) + i·2π/n. The new THE LOOM boss script uses a 32-bullet fixed spiral at 0–6.4 seconds, a 24-bullet aimed/fixed mix at 6.4–12.8 seconds, and a 48-bullet bloom at 12.8–19.2 seconds, ending at 19.2 seconds. The account claims one session, 60 fps on the first run, up to 570 bullets and 16.6 ms per frame. These narrative and performance claims have not been independently checked; corresponding source code, execution traces and measurement records were not obtained. Existing TH04 ring research does not itself validate this new game or its numbers. This entry distinguishes a new game informed by research from a complete reconstruction of Touhou, and does not treat the claimed clean-room, zero-copy process as a legal finding or independent audit.

October 16 candidate snapshot: 3 likes, 194 views; not run here, with performance and time claims attributed to the post.
