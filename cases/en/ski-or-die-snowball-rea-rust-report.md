# Ski or Die snowball mode: a reported REA DOS-game study and Rust/SDL2 rebuild

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-21
Stage: In progress

At 10:44 Beijing time on October 21, 2026, Brian Jhang's four-post introduction describes developer Tuomo Tuunanen's published Ski or Die snowball-mode rebuild: REA analyzes the DOS executable, then Rust and SDL2 recreate movement, snowball trajectories, hits and scoring. The target is one mode, not every mode of the full game.

The report explicitly says audio is unfinished and no side-by-side original-game runtime comparison has been made. The next post lists a project link, but its shortened destination has not been established. Current evidence is a named secondary report, not source inspection or independent reproduction by this collection. The same thread's 3,205 comparisons and 63-byte match for DX-Ball do not validate Ski or Die. Frame rate, completion percentage and feature equivalence are not inferred.
