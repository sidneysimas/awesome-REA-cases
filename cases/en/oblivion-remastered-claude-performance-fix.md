# Oblivion Remastered Performance Fix: reported Claude-assisted engine patches and developer benchmarks

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-21
Stage: Needs verification

At 01:30 Beijing time on October 21, 2026, a post recounts a solo modder's Oblivion Remastered Performance Fix: about 40 engine-level patches targeting CPU work, memory, rendering and world streaming rather than deliberately lowering graphics settings. Claude Code reportedly assisted reverse engineering, patch writing and benchmarking, with humans setting objectives, making decisions and testing gameplay.

Another candidate at 05:16 quotes a report naming the author as LTSharpe. Frustrated with remaster performance, he reportedly subscribed to Claude and directed investigation, construction of a repeatable benchmark and machine-code modifications tested against actual game behavior. The quoted two-week effort reportedly addressed faults involving Unreal, the original Gamebryo engine and AMD frame-generation code. This remains a quotation of reporting; LTSharpe's original logs were not obtained. It is not independent validation or evidence of unguided AI contributions.

Reported developer benchmarks include a 4K Imperial City scene with frame generation enabled, averaging 72 to 102 FPS, described by the source as a 41.7% improvement. Another test's 1% low rose from 26 to 43. Headline claims of up to 48% higher FPS and 90% less stuttering lack independent cross-system support; testing was primarily on one AMD PC. Patch source, exact hardware, logs, game build and visual comparisons were not obtained. No guarantee of unchanged visuals or similar gains is established, nor independent AI repair of a game. REA is not stated; this collection did not run it.
