# Pokemon Yellow: a Claude idea for browser-playable Surfing Pikachu

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:00 and 02:02 Beijing time on October 19, two candidates ask for Claude or Astra to decompile Pokemon Yellow and release Surfing Pikachu as a browser-playable game. The concrete purpose is bringing a surfing experience from a named old game to the browser, not rebuilding the whole Pokemon series.

These are merged as the same specific wish, not two projects. No researcher, code, model invocations, original game version or play tests are supplied. Exaggerated subscription names are not treated as real product specifications. REA use is unconfirmed, and neither started decompilation nor permission to distribute copyrighted assets is established.
