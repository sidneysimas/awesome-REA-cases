# PS2 discs on a jailbroken PS5: a reader's account of AI-assisted emulation

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:08 Beijing time on October 19, a post about backwards compatibility and repeat purchases claims independent developers used AI coding tools and years of open-source emulation research to run original PS2 discs on jailbroken PS5 consoles; PS3 emulation is described as experimental. The purpose is replaying owned discs on newer hardware, not establishing that AI broke PS5 security.

No project, developer, firmware, supported games, actual AI contribution or demonstration logs are supplied. Traditional emulation achievements cannot all be credited to AI, and the inference that commercial motives alone explain missing compatibility is not adopted. This remains an unverified retelling; REA use is unconfirmed, and no console or disc was tested.
