# Super Mario 64: a promotional REA/Opus claim of original movement in a hell scene

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:31 Beijing time on October 19, a promotion claims REA and Opus 5.5 extracted movement, jumps and coin logic from 1996's Super Mario 64, rebuilding a lava, fire and demon scene in a modern engine while retaining its HUD and feel. It claims one evening's work and outlines scanning, extracting numbers, checking them live and porting.

No target version, parameter values, engine name, code or input-trace comparison is supplied. Four lives and 12 coins in a HUD do not establish recovered original logic; zero glitches and identical feel are unverified. The 1996 date, 30th anniversary and nearly 12 million sales are promotional background, not project capabilities or engagement evidence. This is distinct from the existing six-parameter Mario-to-Halo retelling and is not forced into it. No independent execution was performed.
