# A HyperOS monitor: exploring exposed control services

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-15
Stage: In progress

In a discussion of using AI to investigate monitors, Binaryify (@binaryify) recommends first checking DDC/CI control interfaces, then exposed services if unsupported, and says their own monitor runs HyperOS with open ports and services. A third-party post in the October 15, 16:00–20:00 window names Binaryify, saying a Xiaomi monitor lacked DDC/CI so BetterDisplay could not switch inputs, prompting device research with Opus 5.5 and dedicated control software. Rereading the older original confirmed Binaryify as its author, so the information supplements the same target rather than creating another entry. The working software and input-switching motivation come from a third-party account. There is still no model, protocol, code or physical-device acceptance test; 'dedicated software' is not expanded into all control capabilities being verified. The author previously thought the model alone might suffice; REA is unconfirmed, and views about downgrades or bans are not technical facts.

Older original post gives an exploratory route; this window adds a third-party dedicated-client report, not independently checked.
