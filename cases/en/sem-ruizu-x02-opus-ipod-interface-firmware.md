# Ruizu X02: Sem reports Opus firmware extraction, an iPod-style interface and small games

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-26
Stage: Needs verification

At 17:38 Beijing time on October 26, 2026, Sem (@smgdkngt) reported asking Opus to make a cheap Ruizu X02 MP3 player look and work like an iPod 3G. The stock interface had an icon grid, incomplete translations and confusing controls: left/right scrolled while top/bottom went back. He says Claude recreated the Chicago font, iPod menus, a Now Playing screen with a filled progress bar and a color iPod look, remapping up/down to scroll, left to back, and right/center to select.

The author reports AI reading the entire firmware from NAND through USB recovery mode and working out its encrypted update format, UI resources, fonts and key maps. Since the stock updater refused modified firmware, AI wrote an ARM flashing payload, testing patches to stock applications in a Unicorn emulator first. It also reportedly wrote Tetris, Flappy Bird and learning games for a six-year-old from scratch in C within 21 KB RAM, with a Mac simulator running the same code. Learning content reportedly includes 376 words, OpenMoji pictures and the Andika font, in Dutch and English. The author says his role was hardware testing and sending photos of display problems.

Supplementary reading confirmed the author, full reported workflow and presence of images, but their contents were not identified. Firmware revision, source, NAND images, Unicorn logs and verified hardware demonstrations were not obtained. Screen changes, flashing and games are author reports, not this collection's reproduction. REA is not mentioned. No payload was downloaded or executed and no player operated here; status is unverified.
