# Sixteen note files: a reported REA/no-REA decoder comparison

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · REA
Record date: 2026-10-13
Stage: Needs verification

English Daily Insights #129 and the Korean text of the same article recount a comparison in a video: 16 note files, one damaged, with ten-minute attempts both with and without REA. According to the video account, both routes produced decoders handling notes the original application could normally open. The goal is to study a note format and restore reading capability, not recover the entire app. The posts name no software and supply no video URL, samples or validation records. This review did not independently watch or execute the experiment, so the status remains unverified; it establishes neither a speedup nor recovery of damaged files. The two language versions are one report. Calculator, Dinosaur and DX-Ball are existing examples and are not added again.
