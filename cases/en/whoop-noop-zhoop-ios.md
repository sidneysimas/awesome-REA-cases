# WHOOP and Zhoop: an iPhone data path for a health agent

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-15
Stage: Needs verification

An account in this window says Guru canceled a WHOOP subscription after 18 months but wanted to keep using the hardware, so adapted the community NOOP project into a personal iPhone app, Zhoop, receiving heart rate, steps and sleep data and connecting a health AI agent. According to the post, NOOP has studied WHOOP's Bluetooth protocol since June without requiring firmware changes. Guru reused the low-level code, adding diet, calorie-deficit and weight-loss planning and redesigning the UI. Community protocol research is distinguished from the later personal app; underlying reverse engineering is not credited to Guru or AI, and the post's views about AI do not prove NOOP was AI-built. This collection did not check source, health-algorithm accuracy, cost or offline scope. This also differs from the existing WHOOP Android APK analysis record.

Historical NOOP dates come from the account; this window introduces Zhoop rather than newly capturing June work.
