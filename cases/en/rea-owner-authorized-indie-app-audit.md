# Owner-authorized indie-app audits: a REA client-risk reporting proposal

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-20
Stage: In progress

At 03:22 Beijing time on October 20, an author says they use REA for indie developers who request it, rather than copying apps to avoid subscriptions, reporting what an attacker would find first. Proposed checks include hardcoded API keys, client-only premium flags and unauthenticated endpoints. Pricing is $250 per app with 48-hour delivery, the first three free, and anonymized publication only with the owner's approval.

Ten monthly audits yielding $2,500 is the post's business projection, not earned revenue. No named client, target, sample report or delivery record confirms actual vulnerabilities. REA star counts and a Bethesda cease-and-desist are background retellings, not validation of this service. Retained as an in-progress authorized-analysis service proposal; no credentials or apps were accessed.
