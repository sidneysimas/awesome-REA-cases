# Jiuchuan Xiaozhi AI speaker: give Muse stock firmware and hardware specifications

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-17
Stage: In progress

An opening post at 08:21 Beijing time on October 17 proposes flashing Muse firmware onto a Jiuchuan Xiaozhi AI speaker in Muse Wonder. A follow-up at 08:35 describes connecting it to Windows over a USB data cable, identifying the ESP32-S3 serial port, querying flash information and fully backing up stock firmware first. The information and backup are then to be given to Muse to adapt Muse Gadgets' GitHub source to the device's hardware specifications. This supplies concrete device-research inputs and an AI adaptation step, rather than merely proposing an AI speaker. The available text does not identify recovered pins or peripheral settings, or report successful flashing, booting, audio tests or restoration. It remains an ongoing adaptation workflow; suggested commands are not treated as executed results. This collection did not read device firmware or execute those commands.

Candidates describe preparation and an adaptation method only; ESP32-S3 is identified by the text, not independent device inspection.
