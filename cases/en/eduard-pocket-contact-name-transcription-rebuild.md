# Pocket transcription modification: Eduard imports contact names to improve spelling

Category: Applications and clients · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-26
Stage: Needs verification

On October 20, 2026, Eduard Faus Gil (@eduardfausgil) said he had reverse-engineered Pocket and modified its experience in response to ads, microphone quality, name misspellings and a lack of agentic features, attaching a demonstration video. He called it '10X better' without providing a tenfold performance metric.

Supplementary lookup of his same-day discussion shows that the existing workflow could already do word boosting followed by LLM processing, but entering every name manually was inconvenient. He therefore added a simple contact-name import so the system would have most names he might mention. He also clarified that he meant improving name spelling, not identifying speakers without consent. This is a concrete modification of an AI transcription product; it does not establish improved microphone hardware or confirm every advertised capability.

The author said he did not open-source it. No implementation, reverse-engineering method, model, accuracy comparison or independent test was obtained. REA use and AI's participation in the reverse engineering itself remain unknown; the LLM is explicitly part of the transcription workflow. This collection did not inspect the video to validate its contents or run the software. The entry is an unverified author report with tool attribution left unknown, not a measured 10X result. The candidate comes from an earlier window; the supplementary discussion is used only to clarify the specific modification.
