# Samsung TV voice remote: Codez reports connecting Opus-investigated LAN APIs to Grok Bot

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-22
Stage: Needs verification

At 22:07:18 Beijing time on October 22, 2026, Codez (@0xCodez) reports using Opus 5.5 to investigate his Samsung TV and making Grok Bot a voice interface, reportedly opening and browsing Netflix with a spoken request. His proposed workflow has Claude Code use the TV's information to investigate remote-control APIs on the same home network, build a tiny remote exposing only open Netflix, move and select, and connect it to Grok Bot as a plugin. The user must allow the connection on the TV.

The target is connecting existing LAN remote-control capabilities to a voice agent, not removing TV DRM, replacing firmware or breaking Netflix. The author claims support for most recent Samsung, LG and Google TV sets but supplies no particular TV model, firmware, protocol, plugin code, full prompt, test logs or cross-brand demonstration. The advertised range is not a measured compatibility list.
