# FlagRoster's play designer: Olek uses REA to study sideline-coach interactions and gives Claude Code a ten-task plan

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-10-27
Stage: In progress

At 19:05:19 Beijing time on October 27, 2026, Olek Uznański (@OlekUzn) says he used REA to study leading play-design apps, understand why coaches find them easy on the sideline, and produce a ten-task plan Claude Code is working through. A supplementary reading of his full 19:04–19:07 thread identifies FlagRoster, his product for flag-football league rosters, schedules and standings. He wants a sufficiently useful play designer so teams do not need a second app throughout the season. Reference apps are unnamed; neither a particular competitor nor access to server source is inferred.

The thread lists motion, blocks and erase as real tools; shortcuts disabled while typing; formation changes that retain routes; route timing based on distance; option routes, templates and a defense overlay. These are interaction directions the author says emerged from the study, not deployed features individually accepted by this collection. He says the goal was not copying competitors verbatim and recommends reserving original effort for core product work. This does not certify the implementation's legal clean-room status.

The ten-task document, REA output, code, interaction recordings and tests were not obtained. Feature equivalence, weeks saved and user growth are not established. Included as an in-progress study-to-development-plan workflow. Supplementary thread posts supply context rather than additional candidates from this window. Neither FlagRoster nor reference apps were run here.
