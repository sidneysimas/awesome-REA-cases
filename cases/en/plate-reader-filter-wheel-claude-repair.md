Rüdiger Groß (@r_gross_) reports a plate reader that had been out of service for months after clinic technicians abandoned repair. A plate reader measures optical signals from sample plates. Here a filter-wheel alignment error caused startup QC to fail; the author says mechanical and optical checks were normal but a fatal error persisted. They set up a laptop running device software and Claude, used a camera to observe the instrument screen, and returned after lunch to find it working. A reply adds that Claude investigated instrument communication, reset firmware, ran tests and wrote a monitoring script.
