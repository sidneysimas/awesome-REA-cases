# Sony KDL-55W955: a Claude firmware question met with a signing-blocker answer

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 00:55 Beijing time on October 19, @mecetnoca says they asked Claude about changing firmware or making their old TV functional again, receiving an answer that Linux and other factors prevented it. Reading the parent confirms an earlier mention of a 2013 Sony Bravia. At 03:54 the same author, in another conversation, identifies KDL-55W955 and relays Claude's answer that the TV accepts only firmware signed with Sony's private key, with no supported bypass route.

The purpose is extending an old device's life, but the evidence is a user's retelling of a model answer, not boot-chain measurement, signature verification or a discovered vulnerability. No firmware version, full request, analysis logs or tests are supplied. The generalization that Linux TVs cannot be modified is not adopted; no supported route does not establish absolute technical impossibility. REA use is unconfirmed and no firmware was flashed.
