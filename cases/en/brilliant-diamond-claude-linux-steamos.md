# Brilliant Diamond: a claimed Claude audio-library investigation and Windows-to-SteamOS port

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-19
Stage: Needs verification

At 03:25 Beijing time on October 19, an author says they found a Windows-only Brilliant Diamond decompilation project and had Claude port it to Linux, including reversing its audio library. Claude reportedly then created a Flatpak, installed it over SSH on SteamOS and added cover art. The author claims 1920×1080 at 120 fps instead of 720p at 30 fps on a portable PC. The purpose is running an existing rebuild on a personal Linux handheld, not a claim that Claude originally decompiled the whole game.

No repository, audio-library identity, build, device configuration, image/audio comparison or frame-rate record is provided. Resolution, frame rate and success remain author claims, not established stability, full playthrough, original-game compatibility or distribution permission. REA use is unconfirmed; no Flatpak was installed or game run.
