# Armed and Dangerous: exploring remastering approaches with Ghidra and AI

Category: Games and preservation · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-13
Stage: In progress

A poster whose identity is not recorded in the capture says they are remastering the favorite game Armed and Dangerous. They compare two approaches: decompiling with Ghidra and having AI write C functions to replace at runtime, or rewriting with Rust and Bevy. The post says the first route can already load any level and scene from the original game to support subjective improvements and objective accuracy comparisons, but engine behavior such as physics remains constrained by the Havok route. The author hopes to publish a video later. This is an ongoing self-report without a public video or independent verification. REA is not mentioned, so its use is unconfirmed.
