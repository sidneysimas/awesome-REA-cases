# Virtua Fighter 2.1 / 3 TB: AI-assisted decompilation in progress

Category: Games and preservation · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-10-13
Stage: In progress

A project introduction says Virtua Fighter 2.1 (arcade) and Virtua Fighter 3 TB (Dreamcast) are being reverse-engineered with AI coding agents, aiming to reconstruct original logic as readable C/C++, understand internals and eventually port to more platforms. Both are described as WIP, with invitations for contributors experienced in reverse engineering, C/C++, assembly and SEGA development. This supports ongoing AI-assisted game reverse engineering, not completed ports or runnable reconstructions. The post does not establish REA use, which remains unknown.
